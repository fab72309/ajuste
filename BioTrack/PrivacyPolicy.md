# AJUSTE Privacy Policy

AJUSTE stores all user data **locally on device**. No account, no server, no tracking.

If you enable HealthKit, AJUSTE will request permission to read specific categories you approve. Health data are used to prefill metrics; they never leave your device. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications (reminders) are scheduled **locally** on your device after you grant permission.

You may export your data to CSV from the app; exports are saved to a file you control and can share using iOS share sheet.

For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
